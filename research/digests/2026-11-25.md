## 4. MeerKAT, MeerKAT+ and SKA-Mid

1. *Generative Bayesian modelling of radio antenna beams. An application to
   the MeerKAT radio telescope*; Pashapour-Ahmadabadi, Schwarz, de Villiers,
   Basu, Eberle, Enßlin et al., 2026 (A&A, accepted);
   [arXiv:2611.26713](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26713) [abs]. A
   non-parametric information-field-theory beam (NIFTy): a base pattern under
   frequency-dependent scaling and shifting, fitted to the MeerKAT L-band
   array-averaged Stokes I holography; χ²/dof ≈ 1, 400:1 compression,
   about 150 kB per antenna, mean squared deviation more than 10× lower
   than Gaussian, Airy, JimBeam, Eidos and EM simulation; Zernike base for
   any resolution. **ra-ng:** `python/rang/beam_holography.py` samples the
   same de Villiers holography (HolographyBeam) and wraps KatBeam (JimBeam)
   and Eidos; all are L band only, and [uhf-scope.md](../uhf-scope.md)
   section 2.3 marks the beam a **BREAK at UHF** with a 34.9 GB UHF
   holography file. The paper does not say its code or fitted model is
   public. Proposal
   [compressed-beam-uhf](../proposals/compressed-beam-uhf.md). Beam and
   pointing *calibration* (B1-3) stays closed; this is the beam model only.
2. *Mining the time axis with TRON. IV. MeerKAT detects a radio burst from a
   nearby M dwarf*; Ramaila, Smirnov, Myburgh, Buckley, Egbo, Monageng et
   al., 2026 (MNRAS Letters, accepted);
   [arXiv:2611.23447](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23447) [abs]. A
   minute-long, ~100 % circularly polarised 7.7 mJy burst from PM
   J14111−6155 (M1Ve, 19.6 pc) found in SMGPS 1.28 GHz data at 8 s by the
   TRON pipeline; visibility-based dynamic spectra show a drifting,
   ~300 MHz-wide feature cutting off near 1.65 GHz (ECMI); no detection four
   hours later. **ra-ng:** a fourth TRON result, with a circularly polarised
   burst and its dynamic spectrum: a real-data positive control for `rang-core lightcurve` (#9; P2 dynamic spectra in
   [ts-p2.md](../ts-p2.md)), whose real-track bias gates were measured only
   on injected pulses. `lightcurve.py` computes Stokes I only. Proposal
   [stokes-v-lightcurve-tron4](../proposals/stokes-v-lightcurve-tron4.md).
   TRON's data come through a SARAO token (owner decision 2 of
   [v1-timeseries-scope.md](../v1-timeseries-scope.md)).
3. **CARACal** stays at 1.2.1; **katdal** v0.24 (previous digest) [repo].
   **SKAO / SARAO:** web search found one item dated 2026-11, a University of
   Manchester release of 2026-11-17 on MeerKAT mapping hydrogen across
   cosmological scales [page]; no pipeline content in the search summary.

1. *False Periodicities in Repeating Fast Radio Bursts Caused by Burst
   Clustering*; Lan, Wu, Luo, Wang, Dai, 2026 (ApJ, accepted);
   [arXiv:2611.25704](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25704) [abs]. Sliding-window
   period searches on four active repeaters give candidates as significant as
   the reported 1.7 s period of FRB 20201124A; a waiting-time randomisation

2. *Fast Radio Bursts Co-detected by CHIME/FRB and DSA-110 from 400 to 1530
   MHz*; Ocker, Nimmo, Faber, Shin, Ravi, Verdi et al., 2026;
   [arXiv:2611.28250](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28250) [abs], and companion
   *Spectropolarimetric properties of fast radio bursts co-detected by
   CHIME/FRB and DSA-110*; Pandhi, Sherman, Faber, Pleunis, Nimmo, Ocker et
   al., 2026; [arXiv:2611.28251](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28251) [abs]. 15
   co-detections; co-detection is limited by intrinsic width, scattering and

   PA profiles through scatter broadening. **ra-ng:** science; the

   searches. None.
3. *Modelling scintillation in Fast Radio Bursts*; Nicotera, Sprenger,

4. *Gamma-Ray Pulsar Discovery Using a Lattice-Based Method*; Pook-Kolb,

## 8. Agentic and LLM tools

1. *MAASAI: A Tool-Augmented Multi-Agent System for Astronomical Data
   Analysis*; Riggi, Ingallinera, Bordiu, Buemi, Bufano, Burgay et al., 2026;
   [arXiv:2611.31088](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31088) [abs]. Graph-based
   multi-agent system (planning, execution, synthesis workers; retrieval;
   artefact and provenance management; optional human approval; local
   open-weight backends), motivated by SKA-era data; on radio-image
   benchmarks macro-F1 0.53 → 0.72 (extended/diffuse emission) and
   0.37 → 0.88 (morphology) against a commercial-model baseline; a 94-question
   literature benchmark, 59 answers checked by experts. **ra-ng:** the
   closest published system to V2's "agent runs the radio tools"; it works on
   images and catalogues, not visibilities. Its human-approval step is what
   `rang-core mcp` does with confirmed plans (`docs/agents/use-the-mcp-server.md`).
   A matched comparison would need its code (the abstract does not say it is
   public); no proposal.
2. *Integrating AI Coding Agents Into Scientific Research*; Barnes, 2026;
   [arXiv:2611.23334](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23334) [abs] (cs.SE,
   cross-listed astro-ph.IM, submitted 2026-11-17). A six-level PROOF scale
   for AI-assisted numerical results (provenance, reproducibility, openness,
   oversight, falsifiability), from version-controlled human-verified results
   up to independent agent regeneration with attestations; `vaibify`
   (Docker-sequestered agents, file-change monitoring) reaches level 3.
   **ra-ng:** ra-ng's frozen-note, oracle and receipt rule (AGENTS.md
   "Claims") already sits around level 3; the scale is a ready vocabulary for
   the open proposal
   [provenance-checked-handbacks](../proposals/provenance-checked-handbacks.md)
   (cite there when taken up; not a new proposal).
