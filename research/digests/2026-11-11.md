## How this was made

- **Window:** 2024-01 to 2026-11-11, weighted to 2025-11 onwards. The
  previous scan, [literature-2024-2026.md](../literature-2024-2026.md)
  (2026-10-30), covers calibration, RFI, imaging, beams and software up to
  2026-10. Every arXiv ID cited anywhere in `research/` before this digest
  (107, in both the `arXiv:` and the `arxiv.org/abs/` form, the scan's
  included) was removed, so this digest adds to them, not repeats them.
- **Papers:** the arXiv API (`export.arxiv.org/api/query`), 68 keyword and
  name queries in eight areas, date window above, sorted by submission date;
  about 970 hits (author and short-acronym queries returned off-topic noise,
  discarded); about 95 abstracts read, the first 520 characters each, and
  the full abstract for every item whose line below rests on a detail.
  **[abs]** = abstract only, no full text read. Proceedings: arXiv's comments
  field (`co:ADASS`, `co:URSI`, `co:SPIE`, 2025-2026) gave 62 hits, two new
  and relevant (sections 4 and 6). ADS and SKA proceedings were not reached;
  general web search was used for the SKA status only.
- **Software:** GitHub, gitlab.com and git.astron.nl release APIs, read on
  2026-11-11. **[repo]** = release or tag metadata and notes; **[page]** =
  a web page.
- **Verification:** every arXiv ID below was re-queried by `id_list` after
  writing, and its title and first author compared (section "Check").
- **Wall time:** 13:40 to 14:10 (host clock) for search, reading, writing and checks,
  one session, no heavy job; arXiv queries about 5 min of that.

Each item gives: title; authors, year; link; what it does; what it means for
ra-ng, with a file or task.

## 1. RFI flagging (classical and learned)

Context: flagging-by-gain-TV, F2 and F3 are closed or frozen (STATE). The
items below are recorded for a future reopening, not as a reason to reopen.

1. *Detection and excision of radio-frequency interference in radio arrays*;
   V. Ravi, 2026; [arXiv:2611.01308](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01308) [abs].
   Models how primary beam, near-field curvature, bandwidth smearing and fringe
   tracking suppress an emitter in visibilities versus tied-array beams, and
   reduces "antenna-based or array-based detection" to one quantity.
   **ra-ng:** a physical account of why F3 missed wide-band, scan-long,
   antenna-local RFI (0.024 against T's 0.907, [f3-flagger.md](../f3-flagger.md)):
   such RFI may be most detectable per antenna, not per baseline. Read before
   any flagger is reopened.
2. *Scalable Bayesian data curation for next-generation radio experiments*;
   Leeney, de Lera Acedo, Handley, Bevins et al., 2026;
   [arXiv:2606.16525](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16525) [abs]. A latent
   anomaly indicator is marginalised in the likelihood, so curation
   happens inside inference (JAX, GPU) instead of as a pre-flag.
   **ra-ng:** the idea behind proposal
   [mixture-likelihood-gain-solve](../proposals/mixture-likelihood-gain-solve.md),
   applied to `core/src/solve.rs`.
3. *Evaluating the effectiveness of RFI removal algorithms for single pulse
   searches*; Hombal, Levin, Stappers, Droog et al., 2026;
   [arXiv:2601.08351](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08351) [abs]. A benchmark
   of RFI mitigation for RRAT/FRB single-pulse search.
   **ra-ng:** the comparison set for a beamformed product, if V1 ever moves
   past P1 ([vision.md](../vision.md) section 3).
4. **AOFlagger 3.5.0** (gitlab.com release 2026-01-26; the
   [changelog](https://aoflagger.readthedocs.io/en/latest/changelogs/v3.05.html)
   header says 2023-10-06, apparently copied from 3.4) [repo]:
   GUI on Gtkmm 4, Sisco residual display, `normalize_bandpass()` for
   non-complex data, faster winsorized statistics, data with more than 32K
   time steps. No change to the SumThreshold kernel is listed. **ra-ng:**
   [a-flag-aoflagger.md](../a-flag-aoflagger.md)'s oracle version can move to
   3.5 without a kernel recheck; the > 32K time-step fix matters only for
   long native dumps (E2-N).
5. **Tricolour 0.2.1** (2026-04-01) [repo](https://github.com/ratt-ru/tricolour/releases)
   is the version rang already compares against (E1). No newer release.

Already cited in `research/` and so not repeated: Ducharme et al. 2026 (Bayesian
time-series RFI mixture, in the 2026-10 scan) and MARS (Gong et al. 2026, in
[flagging-next.md](../flagging-next.md)).

## 2. Calibration (DD, Bayesian or differentiable, fast solvers)

QuartiCal: no release since v0.2.7 (2026-06-12) [repo]; rang's port stays current.

1. *Solving Self-calibration of ALMA Data with an Optimization Method*;
   Ikeda, Nakazato, Tsukagoshi, Takeuchi et al., 2024;
   [arXiv:2412.03183](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.03183) [abs]. Gains and image
   (PRIISM, sparse modelling) solved as one regularised optimisation, tested
   on ALMA. **ra-ng:** the closest published method to what C5 and C5b
   need: CARACal's loop fails T2 because the unmasked CLEAN model absorbs
   gain errors ([c5-selfcal.md](../c5-selfcal.md),
   [c5b-selfcal-masks.md](../c5b-selfcal-masks.md)). Basis of proposal
   [joint-selfcal-regularised](../proposals/joint-selfcal-regularised.md).
2. *Explainable machine learning workflows for radio astronomical data
   processing*; Yatawatta, Ahmadi, Asabere, Iacobelli et al., 2026;
   [arXiv:2603.16350](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16350) [abs]. A fuzzy
   rule-based (Takagi-Sugeno-Kang) system with deep learning makes
   calibration decisions that astronomers can read; shown on simulations.
   **ra-ng:** prior art for "task procedures for agents" (V2); the
   receipts in `python/rang/core_receipts.py` already record each setting
   such an agent would choose.
3. *Recovering Weak Signals with Normalizing Flows*; S. Yatawatta, 2026;
   [arXiv:2609.06382](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06382) [abs]. Normalizing
   flows restore weak signals that calibration has partly suppressed.
   **ra-ng:** relevant to signal suppression by selfcal
   ([flagging-signal.md](../flagging-signal.md) measured suppression); no
   MeerKAT continuum test.
4. *A Python-Based Peeling Framework for Radio Interferometry (uGMRT 650 MHz)*;
   Peng, An, Zhang, Sekhar et al., 2026;
   [arXiv:2603.10758](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10758) [abs]. DD peeling
   built on CASA tasks. **ra-ng:** a weak baseline for DD work (CASA-based
   peeling); V7's DDECal oracle is the stronger one.
5. **IDG 1.3.0** (git.astron.nl, 2026-08-04) [repo]: new, faster idg-cal
   solver options and a low-memory mode. **ra-ng:** the GPU DD
   calibration oracle for V7 (LOFAR) if DDECal is not enough; GPU-oriented,
   platform support not checked, so oracle, not dependency.
6. **mwa_hyperdrive v0.9.0** (2026-10-30) [repo](https://github.com/MWATelescope/mwa_hyperdrive/releases):
   analytic beams, peel parameter-order fix (v0.8.1). **ra-ng:** still the
   one other Rust calibrator; its peel bug fix is a reminder to test
   parameter order in any rang peel port.
7. *Correlation Calibration: A Hybrid Calibration Technique*; Pascua,
   Sievers, Liu, 2026; [arXiv:2602.06109](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06109)
   [abs]. Hybrid sky/redundant calibration for drift-scan arrays.
   **ra-ng:** not applicable to MeerKAT tracking; recorded so it is not
   re-read next week.

## 3. Imaging and deconvolution

Changed since the last scan: GeMClean now deconvolves (G2-G9), so the scan's
"no deconvolution in scope" no longer holds. DF5-O
([df5o-deconv-oracles.md](../df5o-deconv-oracles.md)) already froze WSClean and
pfb-imaging 0.0.11 as deconvolution oracles on a simulated field; pfb `sara`
at the release's recipe had not converged in 10 major cycles (residual rms
30σ), so the RML side of that comparison is still open.

1. *HyperAIRI: a plug-and-play algorithm for precise hyperspectral image
   reconstruction*; Tang, Dabbech, Jackson, Wiaux, 2025;
   [arXiv:2510.15198](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15198) [abs]. AIRI
   (forward-backward with learned denoisers) extended to wide-band cubes.
   **ra-ng:** the wide-band counterpart to GeMClean's joined channels
   ([g3-spectral.md](../g3-spectral.md)); candidate arm in proposal
   [imaging-gap-comparators](../proposals/imaging-gap-comparators.md).
2. *Generative imaging for radio interferometry with fast uncertainty
   quantification*; Mars, Liaudat, Whitney, Betcke et al., 2025;
   [arXiv:2507.21270](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21270) [abs]. RI-GAN: a
   conditional GAN with the measurement operator in the network takes the
   dirty image and PSF and draws approximate posterior samples (UQ) cheaply.
   **ra-ng:** UQ for GeMClean images ([uncertainty-coverage.md](../uncertainty-coverage.md));
   the abstract does not say it was tested on real data.
3. *SIFARI: Self-Supervised Interferometric Fitting for Astronomical Radio
   Imaging*; Mao, Isella, Perdikaris, Lo et al., 2026;
   [arXiv:2609.35966](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35966) [abs]. A neural field
   fits visibilities directly, self-supervised, without hand-set priors.
   **ra-ng:** ALMA-type data; a later arm for the comparator proposal, not
   first.
4. *POLISH'ing the Sky: Wide-Field and High-Dynamic Range Interferometric
   Image Reconstruction*; Wu, Connor, McCarty, Bouman, 2026;
   [arXiv:2603.09162](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09162) [abs]. POLISH
   extended with patch-wise training and stitching (wide field) and an arcsinh
   intensity transform (dynamic range), tested on T-RECS simulations.
   **ra-ng:** states the dynamic-range limits of
   learned imagers; a GeMClean comparison would need its released weights.
5. *To CLEAN or not to CLEAN: Data Processing in the ngVLA era*; H. Müller,
   2025; [arXiv:2509.15176](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15176) [abs]. Review
   of CLEAN against RML, compressive sensing and Bayesian imaging for
   next-generation data volumes. **ra-ng:** the reference list for
   [clean-overview.md](../clean-overview.md).
6. *Accelerating radio astronomy imaging with RICK: a step towards SKA-Mid and
   SKA-Low*; Lacopo, De Rubeis, Gheller, Taffoni et al., 2026;
   [arXiv:2601.19714](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19714) [abs]. Portable
   distributed gridding and FFT (HeFFTe) on CPUs and GPUs. **ra-ng:** a
   GPU/CPU-portable gridder to compare against the wgpu path
   ([x2-wgpu.md](../x2-wgpu.md)) on speed at matched accuracy.
7. *astroCAMP: A Community Benchmark and Co-Design Framework for Sustainable
   SKA-Scale Radio Imaging*; Constantinescu, Rodríguez Álvarez, Morin,
   Orliac et al., 2025; [arXiv:2512.13591](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13591)
   [abs]. Reports 4-14 % of hardware peak in current pipelines and proposes
   shared metrics with fidelity tolerances. **ra-ng:** a public yardstick for
   [gemclean-speed.md](../gemclean-speed.md); adopt its metric names if they
   fit.
8. *Million Points of Light (MPoL): a PyTorch library for radio
   interferometric imaging and inference*; Czekala, Jennings, Zawadzki, Nizam
   et al., 2025; [arXiv:2502.00100](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.00100) [abs];
   v0.3.1 2026-05-25 [repo](https://github.com/MPoL-dev/MPoL/releases).
   Differentiable RML imaging. **ra-ng:** an RML oracle that installs on all
   platforms (pure Python), unlike pfb-imaging; useful for small cases.
