## How this was made

- **Window:** first versions submitted 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-17 (the
  2026-11-10 submissions were announced after the previous digest's
  queries ran), and releases published 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-18.
- **Deviation from the skill's step 3.** The arXiv query API
  (`export.arxiv.org/api/query`) returned HTTP 429 to every request from
  07:58 onward, including after back-off of 30-180 s; no query completed.
  Instead the whole `physics:astro-ph` set was harvested through arXiv's
  OAI-PMH endpoint (`oaipmh.arxiv.org`, `ListRecords`, `from=2026-11-11`,
  arXiv metadata format, full abstracts): 1,706 records, 947 of them with a
  latest version dated 2026-11-10 or later. The skill's keyword queries for
  all eight areas and the proceedings comment-field queries (ADASS, URSI,
  SPIE plus "radio") were then applied as case-insensitive regular
  expressions to title plus abstract, with `astro-ph.IM` required where the
  skill requires it: 9 hits, 7 unique papers. Because that is thin, all 174
  `astro-ph.IM` titles in the window and the radio-keyword titles in the
  other astro-ph categories (16) were also read by eye; 22 full abstracts
  were read. **Not searched this week:** papers listed only in cs, eess or
  physics categories (the skill's `agent AND LLM` query is not restricted to
  astro-ph); a cs.CL paper cross-listed to astro-ph.IM was found.
- **Dates.** OAI's `created` field is the latest version's date, not v1's.
  Every arXiv ID below was fetched again by `GetRecord` in `arXivRaw` format
  (independent of the harvest) for its version history, title and authors;
  four candidates were revisions of earlier papers and are labelled so.
- **Repeats.** 174 arXiv IDs cited anywhere in `research/` (both link forms)
  were removed; none of this week's candidates was among them.
- **Software:** GitHub releases and tags, gitlab.com and git.astron.nl v4
  release APIs, PyPI JSON for pfb-imaging, all read on 2026-11-18.
  **[repo]** = release or tag notes; **[page]** = a web page. SKAO and SARAO
  news by web search only (no API): nothing dated 2026-11 found.
- **Tags:** **[abs]** = abstract only, no full text read.
- **Wall time:** 07:57 to about 08:35 (host clock, NST), one session, no
  heavy job; about 6 min of that was the failed API attempts, 1 min the
  OAI-PMH harvest.

Each item gives: title; authors, year; link; what it does; what it means for

## 3. Imaging and deconvolution

1. **WSClean 3.8** (gitlab.com, 2026-11-17)
   [changelog](https://wsclean.readthedocs.io/en/latest/changelogs/v3.8.html)
   [page]. Requires EveryBeam ≥ 0.9.0. Among the changes: multiscale
   cleaning does "far fewer FFTs" and its residual update "no longer wraps
   PSF sidelobes around the image"; `-multiscale-fits-mask` gives per-scale
   clean masks; wgridder recentering; an updated ducc0 wgridder; a
   specialised BDA wgridder; RM-space deconvolution (`-rm-deconvolution`,
   with multiscale); squared-channel joining for polarimetric multiscale; a
   fix for cross-polarisation leakage with `-diagonal-visibilities`; an
   in-memory imaging API. **ra-ng:** every WSClean comparison in `research/`
   is against 3.7 (`rang-wsclean-native:3.7`):
   [gemclean-speed.md](../gemclean-speed.md) (GeMClean faster at `-j 16`
   on all seven skies, WSClean faster at `-j 1` on H1-H3),
   [gemclean-ms-mask.md](../gemclean-ms-mask.md) (WSClean diverged on RM and
   PM), [multiscale-flux-fix.md](../multiscale-flux-fix.md) and
   [pol-join-squared.md](../pol-join-squared.md) (section 7 already expects
   3.8's theoretic beam to differ). The multiscale speed and residual-update
   changes bear directly on those numbers. Proposal
   [wsclean-3-8-rebaseline](../proposals/wsclean-3-8-rebaseline.md).

## 6. Pulsars, FRBs and GRBs

1. **PRESTO v6.3.0-v6.3.3** (2026-11-14 to 2026-11-17)
   [repo](https://github.com/scottransom/presto/releases). 6.3.0:
   `prepfold -usepoly` (tempo2 polycos; automatic for folds over 2 h);
   polyco folding in `prepfold_multi`; a fix for known-pulsar matching that

   oracle run: the `-ncpus` dependence and the MeerKAT PSRFITS matching bug

   Statistically Controlled Screening on CPUs and GPUs*; Nitz, Kacanja,

4. *Combining Pulsar Timing data with MetaPulsar. The linearized timing
